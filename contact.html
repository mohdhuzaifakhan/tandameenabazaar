Yes! Basic shop listing on Digital Meena Bazaar is completely free. Advanced features and premium plans may be available in the future.

When a customer clicks "Order on WhatsApp" on any product, they are redirected directly to your WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. You then handle the order conversation directly with them.
